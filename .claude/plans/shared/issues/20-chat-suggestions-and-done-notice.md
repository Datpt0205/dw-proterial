# 20 — Next questions after each answer; a notice when an answer lands while the widget is closed

Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: none
Category: chat

## What

§1: "Câu gợi ý tiếp theo sau mỗi câu trả lời (hiện chỉ có khi chat trống)"
and "Báo khi câu trả lời xong lúc widget đang đóng (hiện chỉ có dấu chưa
đọc)". Checked 2026-11-27: neither built. `AssistantChat` shows `examples`
only on an empty conversation; the widget marks a conversation unread
(`apps/web/app/sales/_components/assistant-widget.tsx:288-294`) and says
nothing else.

## Design

- **Suggestions are code's, from the case:** the turn view gains
  `suggestions: list[str]` (at most 3), chosen by code from the case's state
  (what `next_steps.py` already offers for it) and the kinds the chat
  answers, worded in the tenant's copy (`configs/copy/sales_assistant@…`,
  new version). Never generated by the model (no free text offered as a
  button). The frame draws them under the last answer with antd X `Prompts`;
  a press asks it.
- **Done while closed:** when an answer finishes and the widget is closed,
  `App.useApp().notification` says "DW1 đã trả lời: <case>" with "Mở" that
  opens the widget at that conversation; the unread mark stays. Nothing when
  the widget is open or the person is on that case's page.

## Acceptance

- Unit: suggestions follow the case state, at most 3, from the copy.
- Vitest: Prompts render after an answer and ask on press; the notification
  only when closed; "Mở" opens the right conversation.

## Owed

- none.
